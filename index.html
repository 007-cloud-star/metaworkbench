Meta Workbench

Tools & field notes for people who run meta-analyses

Meta Workbench is a small, free toolkit built by a clinician-researcher who does systematic reviews after night shifts. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — no sign-up, no uploads, no watermarks. 工具全部免费、在浏览器本地运行，不用注册。

Free tools

PRISMA Flow Diagram Generator

Enter your identification, screening and inclusion counts and download a publication-ready PRISMA 2020 flow diagram as PNG. Counts are cross-checked automatically.

Live Open tool

Forest Plot Maker

Paste study-level results and get a forest plot with fixed-effect and DerSimonian–Laird random-effects pooling, I² and τ² — validated against published analyses before release.
