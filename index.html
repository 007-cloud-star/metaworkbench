Meta Workbench

Tools & field notes for people who run meta-analyses

Meta Workbench is a small, free toolkit built by a clinician-researcher who does systematic reviews after night shifts. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — no sign-up, no uploads, no watermarks. 工具全部免费、在浏览器本地运行，不用注册。

Free tools

PRISMA Flow Diagram Generator

Enter your identification, screening and inclusion counts and download a publication-ready PRISMA 2020 flow diagram as PNG. Counts are cross-checked automatically.

Live Open tool

Forest Plot Generator

Paste study-level results and get a forest plot with fixed-effect and DerSimonian–Laird random-effects pooling, I² and τ². Pooling code validated against an independent Python calculation.

Live Open tool

RoB 2 Traffic-Light Plot

Turn your risk-of-bias judgements into the traffic-light and summary plots journals expect, without fighting R code. Overall judgements follow the RoB 2 rule; summary bars can be weighted by sample size.

Live Open tool

Sample Size Calculator

The common comparative designs — two means, two proportions, non-inferiority — with the formula and an attrition adjustment shown, not hidden.
