Who it’s for

  • Clinicians and graduate students planning a first or second systematic review of intervention trials
  • Research teams who need genuine two-reviewer discipline without paying for a screening platform
  • Anyone whose last review ended in a spreadsheet graveyard and a PRISMA diagram that didn’t add up

What it is not

Not a service. No done-for-you meta-analysis, no manuscript writing, no consulting bundled with a template purchase.

No guaranteed publication — or acceptance, or any particular journal outcome. The pack sells process discipline, nothing more.

Not a statistics course. The analysis starter assumes you know what a random-effects model is.

Not for network meta-analysis, diagnostic-accuracy or observational-evidence reviews — the forms are built for intervention RCTs.

Templates cannot make eligibility, extraction or bias judgements for you. The work is still yours; the pack makes it auditable.
