How the pooling works

  1. Standard errors from your CIs. Each study's standard error is recovered from its 95% CI: SE = (upper − lower) / (2 × 1.96), computed on the log scale for odds and risk ratios, and on the raw scale for mean differences.
  2. Fixed-effect. Studies are weighted by the inverse of their variance (w = 1/SE²). The pooled estimate is the weighted mean, with SE = √(1/Σw).
  3. Heterogeneity. Cochran's Q is the weighted sum of squared deviations from the fixed-effect estimate. I² expresses the share of variation due to heterogeneity rather than chance, and the DerSimonian–Laird estimator turns the excess of Q over its degrees of freedom into τ², the between-study variance.
  4. Random-effects. Each study's variance is inflated by τ² (w* = 1/(SE² + τ²)) and the weighted mean is recomputed. When τ² = 0 the two models coincide.

Frequently asked questions

Which effect measures are supported?
Odds ratios and risk ratios (pooled on the log scale, null line at 1) and mean differences / standardised mean differences (pooled on the raw scale, null line at 0). Enter the effect and its 95% CI exactly as reported by your study or your extraction sheet.

Fixed-effect or random-effects — which should I report?
That is a design decision, not a software default: fixed-effect assumes one true effect; random-effects allows the true effect to vary between studies and is the usual choice when populations or protocols differ. This tool shows both, plus I² and τ², so you can justify your choice. Pre-specify the primary model in your protocol.

Is this generator free?
Yes. It is free to use and the PNG download carries no watermark and requires no account.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?
No. The plot is drawn locally in your browser; nothing is sent to a server.

Can I trust the numbers?
The statistics were validated against an independent Python implementation (numpy/scipy hand formulas) on the preloaded demo dataset, matching to at least three significant figures. Even so, always verify pooled figures against your primary analysis software before submission — this tool is a drafting and checking aid, not a replacement for your registered analysis plan.
